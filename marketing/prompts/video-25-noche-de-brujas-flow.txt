==================== CLIP 1 · "El círculo" (8 s) ====================
Vertical 9:16, cinematic close-up, Halloween night, warm and magical candlelight, cozy and mystical, not scary. A dark
wooden table covered with a deep indigo velvet cloth. Only a woman's hands are visible (elegant, with a thin gold ring
and a violet sleeve with golden embroidery). The hands slowly pour coarse white salt from a small brass bowl, drawing a
complete circle on the velvet. Inside the circle stand two candles: one white and one violet. The hands strike a match
and light the white candle, then the violet one; the flames rise softly. Around the circle: sprigs of fresh rosemary,
a few bright orange marigold flowers, and in the soft-focus background a small carved pumpkin glowing warmly from the
inside, with a light mist drifting near the table. Slow, smooth camera push-in toward the two flames.
Sound: the scrape of the match, crackling flames, a soft breeze. No music, no voices.
No faces, no text, no letters, no logos. Warm, golden, serene, nothing frightening.

==================== CLIP 2 · "Lo que se va" (8 s, Extender desde el clip 1) ====================
Continue the same scene: same table, same circle of salt, same white and violet candles, same pumpkin and marigolds,
same hands and sleeve. The hands hold a small folded piece of paper, bring one corner to the flame of the white candle
and place it on a small ceramic plate inside the circle, where it burns gently; a thin ribbon of smoke rises and
curls toward the candles. Then the hands place one orange marigold flower in front of the circle, as an offering,
and withdraw. For the last 3 seconds: a fixed, static shot of the whole table from slightly above: the salt circle,
the two candles burning, the plate with ashes, the marigolds and the glowing pumpkin in soft focus behind, with gentle
mist; the camera does not move and no hands are in frame.
Sound: paper crackling as it burns, flames, a soft breeze. No music, no voices.
No faces, no text, no letters, no logos. The paper burns calmly on the plate (safe, small flame).

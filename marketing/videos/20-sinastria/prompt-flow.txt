==================== CLIP 1 · "Dos cielos" (8 s) ====================
Vertical 9:16, cinematic, warm and romantic night. [COUPLE]. They lie side by side on a blanket on a rooftop terrace
at night, string lights glowing softly around them and the city lights far below. They look up at a sky full of
stars, she points at something and they both smile. Slowly, thin lines of golden light draw two separate
constellations in the sky above them, one over each of them, like two delicate star maps made of light. The two
constellations drift toward each other and gently overlap, and where their lines cross, small sparks of gold
appear. The camera slowly rises from their faces to the sky. Soft, magical, hopeful atmosphere.
Sound: distant city at night, crickets, a soft laugh, no music, no dialogue.
No text, no letters, no zodiac symbols, no numbers, no logos.

==================== CLIP 2 · "Los hilos" (8 s) ====================
Vertical 9:16, same couple, same rooftop, same night and light. [COUPLE]. They sit up close together on the blanket,
she holds a phone between them and they look at the glowing screen, curious and amused (the screen only shows a soft
golden circular pattern, nothing readable). From the phone two circles of golden light rise into the air, one in
front of each of them, and thin glowing threads stretch between the two circles, some warm green, some amber, softly
pulsing. He looks at her, she laughs and leans her head on his shoulder; the threads of light wrap gently around
their intertwined hands and glow brighter. Slow push-in to their hands and the golden threads. Warm, intimate,
joyful atmosphere.
Sound: soft night ambience, a quiet laugh, no music, no dialogue.
No text, no letters, no readable screen, no zodiac symbols, no numbers, no logos.

Negativo (agrégalo al final si algo sale raro):

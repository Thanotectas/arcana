Slow, smooth camera push-in from the whole table to the hands holding the egg.

No faces (frame from the shoulders down), no text, no letters, no logos. Warm, golden, calm, nothing dark or scary.

==================== CLIP 3 · «Romper y esperar» (8 s, Extender desde el clip 2) ====================
Continue the same scene. The hands bring the egg to the clear glass of water and gently crack it on the rim, at water

sinks slowly to the bottom, and thin translucent threads of egg white rise slowly upward like three little candles,

the yolk at the bottom and the rising threads clearly visible; the camera does not move and no hands are in frame.
Sound: the soft crack of the shell, a gentle splash, candles. No music, no voices.
No faces, no text, no letters, no logos. The egg looks natural and clean (no blood, nothing dark or frightening).

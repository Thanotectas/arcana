==================== CLIP 1 · «El libro» (8 s) ====================
Vertical 9:16, cinematic close-up, warm candlelight, nostalgic and mysterious atmosphere, like a memory. On an old
dark wooden table: a thick antique leather book, a brass candlestick with a lit candle, an old folded map, a few
pressed dried flowers and a small brass pocket watch. Only a young woman's hands are visible (slender, a thin gold
ring, the sleeve of a soft cream knitted sweater). The hands slowly open the book and turn a yellowed page; tiny golden
dust particles float up into the candlelight. The hands stop on a page with a pressed flower and rest there, as if
remembering something. Slow, smooth camera push-in toward the hands and the page.
Sound: a page turning, the candle crackling, the soft ticking of the pocket watch. No music, no voices.
No faces, no readable text on the pages (only blurred old handwriting), no letters, no logos. Warm, golden, serene.

==================== CLIP 2 · «Las fotos» (8 s, Extender desde el clip 1) ====================
Continue the same scene: same table, book, candle, map, pocket watch, same hands and cream sleeve. The hands close the
book gently and open a small carved wooden box next to it. Inside: old sepia photographs, a vintage iron key and a
ribbon. The hands lift one sepia photograph of an old city street from another century (blurred, no faces visible) and
hold it toward the candle, as if recognizing the place. The candle flame leans softly toward the photo for a moment.
Slow camera movement, close-up on the photograph in the hands.
Sound: the creak of the wooden box, paper, the candle, the pocket watch. No music, no voices.
No faces (the people in the photograph are tiny and blurred or seen from behind), no readable text, no logos.

==================== CLIP 3 · «La ventana» (8 s, Extender desde el clip 2) ====================
Continue the same scene. The hands place the sepia photograph on the open map, next to the iron key, and slowly
withdraw out of frame. The camera rises gently and reveals, behind the table, a window with old wooden frames: outside,
a big full moon in a deep blue night sky, with soft clouds. For the last 3 seconds: a fixed, static shot of the whole
table (candle burning, book, wooden box, photograph, map, key, pocket watch) with the full moon in the window behind;
the camera does not move and no hands are in frame.
Sound: the candle, a soft night breeze, the pocket watch. No music, no voices.
No faces, no readable text, no logos. Warm candlelight inside, cool blue moonlight outside.

==================== SI ALGO SALE MAL ====================
- Aparece una cara: agrega «extreme close-up of the hands only, the person is out of frame».
- Aparecen letras legibles en el libro o en las fotos: «only blurred old handwriting, nothing readable».
- La foto sepia muestra caras: «an empty old city street seen from far away, no people».
- La luna sale rara o doble: genera solo el plano final aparte (4–8 s), «fixed static shot, one full moon in the window».
- Sale oscuro o tenebroso: «warm golden candlelight, cozy and nostalgic, nothing scary».

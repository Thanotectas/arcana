==================== CLIP 1 · «La casa de café» (8 s) ====================
Vertical 9:16, cinematic, warm golden light through latticed wooden windows, 16th-century Ottoman coffeehouse
atmosphere. A low brass tray on a carved wooden table, a copper cezve (small long-handled coffee pot) bubbling gently
on hot coals, steam rising, small white porcelain cups without handles, a brass incense burner with a thin smoke line,
patterned kilim rugs and cushions in the background, softly out of focus. Slow dolly-in toward the cezve as the foam
rises to the rim. Only hands visible (an older hand with a simple silver ring) lifting the cezve and pouring thick dark
coffee with foam into a cup.
Sound: coals crackling, coffee bubbling, distant murmur of conversation, a spoon touching porcelain. No music, no voices.
No faces, no readable text, no logos, no modern objects.

==================== CLIP 2 · «Voltear la taza» (8 s) ====================
Vertical 9:16, cinematic close-up, warm candlelight, intimate kitchen-table atmosphere. A small porcelain coffee cup
with a handle on its saucer, almost empty, thick coffee grounds at the bottom. A woman's hands (a thin gold bracelet)
place the saucer on top of the cup like a lid, hold it for a moment as if making a wish, then turn the cup and saucer
over in one smooth movement so the cup rests upside down on the saucer. One hand places a small gold coin on top of the
upside-down cup. Slow push-in.
Sound: porcelain clinking, a soft breath, a coin tapping porcelain. No music, no voices.
No faces, no readable text, no logos.

==================== CLIP 3 · «Las figuras» (8 s) ====================
Vertical 9:16, cinematic macro shot, warm light. The hands lift the cup and tilt it toward the camera: inside, the dried
coffee grounds have formed dark organic shapes on the white porcelain walls — the clear silhouette of a bird in flight
near the rim, a winding path, and a small heart shape near the handle. Light glides slowly over the shapes; tiny golden
particles of light shimmer briefly around the bird shape. Very slow rotation of the cup.
Sound: porcelain, a quiet room, a soft magical shimmer when the light touches the bird. No music, no voices.
No faces, no readable text or letters, no logos.

==================== CLIP 4 · «La sobremesa» (8 s) ====================
Vertical 9:16, cinematic, warm candlelight, cozy table at night. Two small coffee cups turned upside down on their
saucers, a cezve, a plate of Turkish delight, a glass of water and a lit candle on an embroidered tablecloth. Two pairs
of hands (one older, one younger) rest close to each other on the table, as if sharing a secret, then slowly withdraw.
For the last 3 seconds: a fixed, static shot of the table with the upside-down cups and the candle; no hands, no movement
except the candle flame.
Sound: candle, a soft laugh far away, porcelain. No music, no voices.
No faces, no readable text, no logos.

==================== VOZ DE SIBILA (ElevenLabs, Marcela, misma configuración) ====================
(~750 caracteres, ~50 s. Los «…» son pausas: déjalos.)
Bienvenida a la Biblioteca de Sibila. Hoy: la lectura del café…
En el siglo dieciséis, el café llegó a Estambul, y con él nacieron las primeras casas de café del Imperio otomano. Allí empezó una costumbre que hoy la UNESCO protege como patrimonio de la humanidad…
Se bebe despacio, pensando en una pregunta. Se tapa la taza con el plato, se pide un deseo… y se voltea…
Cuando la porcelana se enfría, la borra dibuja figuras. Un pájaro trae noticias. Un camino, un viaje. Un corazón cerca del asa… amor que se acerca a tu casa…
De Estambul a Beirut, de Atenas a Buenos Aires, alguien sigue preguntando: ¿te leo la taza?…
Mándame la foto de la tuya en mi arcana punto com. Yo te la leo.
